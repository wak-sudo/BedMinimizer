This folder contains the engineering thesis that provides the theoretical foundation for how this project works. The entire thesis was written over a period of two months, working full-time.

It is written in Polish, but language models should be able to handle the translation without much difficulty. If you have any questions, feel free to reach out.

Due to the limited time available, I was not able to:

* conduct a deeper analysis of methods for generating automata with given parameters, experimentally evaluate them, or propose a fairer generation procedure,
* carry out higher-quality experiments,
* formulate a proof of the soundness and completeness of the encoding.

Additionally, some interesting directions for further development include:

* considering an encoding that takes the experimental results into account, particularly the importance of the parameter *m*,
* carrying out a more concrete analysis of the lower bounds for this problem encodings.

I believe there is enough material left here for at least two more diploma theses.
